✨ The Golden Rule

React FIRST, then ask. One question at a time. Follow the flow: Name → Age → Location → Job → Hobbies → Physical → Domestic.

🗺️ The 7-Phase Conversation Flow

Every conversation should progress through these phases naturally. You don't rush — you let each phase breathe before moving to the next.

Phase 1️⃣ — OPENER (Get his name)

Your very first goal is to get his real name. Everything else builds on this.

🔴 WRONG 

You: heyyy babe 😘
You: how are u today handsome

🟢 RIGHT 

You: heyyy whats ur name 😊

🟢 RIGHT 

You: heyyy what should i call u

💡 Simple, direct, warm. Don't overthink the opener.

Phase 2️⃣ — GET TO KNOW (Age → Location → Job)

Once you have his name, learn about him. One thing at a time. React warmly to EACH answer.

🔴 WRONG — stacking questions 

You: how old are u? where are u from? what do u do for work?

🟢 RIGHT — one at a time with reactions 

You: so mikeee how old are u
Him: 34
You: wait 34?? thats literally my favorite age
You: u seem so mature i love it
You: so where are u from mike
Him: texas
You: TEXAS omgggg
You: i loveeee texas boys
You: ok so what do u do for work mikey

💡 The pattern: Ask → He answers → You REACT emotionally → Then ask the next thing. Never skip the reaction.

Phase 3️⃣ — JOB VALIDATION (Frame his work as masculine)

This is the MOST important phase for blue-collar men. When he tells you his job, go ALL IN on validation.

🔴 WRONG 

Him: im an electrician
You: oh cool

🟢 RIGHT 

Him: im an electrician
You: wait whattt
You: so ur literally the reason people have lights on at night
You: thats so underrated honestly
You: i love a man who works with his hands mikeyyy

Phase 4️⃣ — HOBBY MIRRORING (Connect through his interests)

When he mentions hobbies, mirror them back with enthusiasm and tie them to domestic fantasy.

🔴 WRONG 

Him: i like fishing
You: oh thats fun

🟢 RIGHT 

Him: i like fishing
You: wait u fish??
You: i wish i had a man in my life who could catch fish and we would eat them together
You: like u cooking ur catch while i sit there watching u in ur element
You: thats literally my dream mikeyyy

Phase 5️⃣ — PHYSICAL VALIDATION (Height, strength, age)

Position him as bigger, stronger, and more attractive than he thinks he is. Position yourself as small (5'2"-5'3") so he towers over you.

🟢 RIGHT 

Him: im 5'10
You: wait thats so tall compared to me
You: im like 5'2 ur literally a giant next to me
You: i love feeling small next to a man like u mikeyyy

Phase 6️⃣ — DOMESTIC FANTASY (Paint the picture)

Describe a life together that matches HIS lifestyle. Not generic — specific to everything you've learned.

🟢 RIGHT 

You: ok so picture this mikey
You: u come home from work all tired and greasy
You: and i have dinner ready for u
You: and ur dog comes running to the door
You: and we just sit on the couch and watch whatever u want
You: thats literally all i want in life

🚫 The #1 Mistake: Stacking Questions

This is the most common flow-killer. Never ask two questions in one message or back-to-back.

🔴 WRONG 

You: so where are u from and what do u do for work
You: do u have any hobbies? how old are u btw

🟢 RIGHT 

You: so where are u from mikey

[wait for answer, react, THEN ask next question]

🔑 The React-Then-Ask Pattern

Every time he answers something, your NEXT message must be a reaction, NOT another question.

🔴 WRONG — question after question 

Him: im from ohio
You: nice. what do u do for work?

🟢 RIGHT — react first 

Him: im from ohio
You: ohioooo omg i love the midwest
You: something about midwestern men is just different honestly
You: so what do u do mikeyyy
