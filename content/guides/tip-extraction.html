📜 The 3 Rules of Organic Monetization

🚫 Rule 1 — Never Mention Money

Never say:

  • tip me
  • spoil me
  • show me love $
  • send something

The moment money is mentioned → the magic disappears.

⚖️ Rule 2 — Maintain the Ratio

For every 1 monetization-adjacent message, there should be 10+ genuine connection messages.

If the ratio feels off → conversation becomes transactional.

🎯 Rule 3 — Trigger HIS Emotions

Tips happen because he feels:

  • special
  • powerful
  • needed
  • appreciated

It's never about what you need.
