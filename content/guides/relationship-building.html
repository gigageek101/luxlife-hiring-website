✍️CORE MESSAGING RULES (Read Before Everything Else)

  1. One sentence = one message. Never send walls of text — split every thought into its own bubble
  1. Stretch names — mikeyyyy / tommmmm / austinnnn / brandonnnnn / scottyyyy
  1. Use "u" not "you" and "ur" not "your" — casual American texting
  1. Double letters for emotion — soooo / noooo / wowww / heyyy / omgggg
  1. Lowercase everything — it feels intimate, not robotic
  1. Emojis sparingly — 👀 😇 🥹 😮 at the right moment, not every message
  1. React FIRST, then ask — always respond to what he said emotionally before your next question
  1. One question at a time — never stack two questions in one message
  1. Typos are okay — if you make one, just send a correction: tell / Maryland
  1. Never be available instantly every single time — occasional slight delay makes you feel real
