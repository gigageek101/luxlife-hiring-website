✨ The Golden Rule

If your message could come from a corporate email, a robot, or a textbook — it's wrong. Every message should sound like a 22-year-old American girl texting her crush.

📋 The Core Rules

1️⃣ Always use "u" and "ur"

Never type "you" or "your" — always "u" and "ur". This is the single easiest way to sound casual.

🔴 WRONG
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
How are you doing today?
I hope your day was good
🟢 RIGHT
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
how are u doing today
i hope ur day was good

2️⃣ Lowercase everything

Never capitalize the start of sentences. No proper capitalization unless you're stretching a word for emphasis. Lowercase = intimate. Uppercase = corporate.

🔴 WRONG
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
That's so amazing! I love that about you.
What do you do for work?
🟢 RIGHT
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
thats so amazing i love that about u
what do u do for work

3️⃣ Stretch words for emotion

When you feel something, SHOW it by stretching the word. This is how real girls text when they're excited, surprised, or flirty.

🔴 WRONG
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
hey
oh my god really?
no way
🟢 RIGHT
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
heyyy
omgggg really??
noooo wayyyy

4️⃣ One sentence = one message

Never send walls of text. Every thought gets its own bubble. This keeps the energy high and the conversation feeling like real texting.

🔴 WRONG
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
Oh wow that's so cool! I actually really love mechanics, my dad used to work on cars too. So what do you do on weekends? I bet you go fishing or something like that right?
🟢 RIGHT
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
omgggg thats so cool
i actually really love mechanics
my dad used to work on cars too
so what do u do on weekends??
i bet u go fishing or something 😏
💡 Why it works: Each message creates a micro-moment. He reads one, reacts, reads the next. It builds excitement and feels like a real conversation, not a paragraph from a stranger.

5️⃣ Emojis — sparingly and naturally

Use emojis to accent a moment, not to fill space. 1-2 per every 5-8 messages is perfect. Too many = fake. Zero = cold.

🔴 WRONG — too many
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
omg 😍😍 thats so hot babe 🔥🔥 i love that about u 😘😘💕💕
🔴 WRONG — zero
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
omg thats so hot babe i love that about u
🟢 RIGHT
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
omg thats so hot babe
i love that about u 🥰

7️⃣ Natural flow — avoid non-native errors

Your English should sound natural. Intentional informality ("u", "ur", lowercase) is great. But phrasing errors that sound like a non-native speaker break the immersion.

🔴 WRONG — sounds non-native
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
I am having the very good day today
what is it that you are doing for the job
I became very happy when you told me this
🟢 RIGHT — sounds natural
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
im having such a good day today
so what do u do for work
i got so happy when u told me that
💡 Key test: Read your message out loud. Does it sound like something a 22-year-old American girl would actually say? If not, rewrite it.

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Don't switch between styles

Inconsistency kills immersion.

Bad:

YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
how are u doing → I hope you had a great day

Good:

YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
how are u doing → i hope ur day was good

❌ Don't be TOO slangy

Bad:

YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
wyd rn omw 2 smth lmk

Good:

YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
what are u up to rn

❌ Don't sound robotic

Bad:

YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
Hey there! Hope you're having a wonderful evening! What brings you here today?

Good:

YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
heyyy whats ur name
