✨ The Golden Rule

A “no” is never the end of the conversation — it's a redirect.

When a fan says:

  • “not now”
  • goes quiet after a PPV

🔄 The 4-Step Objection Framework

Step 1 — ✅ ACKNOWLEDGE

Accept his response immediately.

Examples:

  • “omg totally get it”
  • “no worries at all babe”
  • “thats completely ok”

Step 2 — 🔁 REFRAME

Make it about connection, not money.

Examples:

  • “i just wanted u to see it first”
  • “i wasnt even thinking about the price honestly”

Step 3 — ↪️ REDIRECT

Return to personal conversation.

Examples:

  • “anyways tell me about ur day”
  • “did u end up going to that car show?”

Step 4 — 🎯 RE-APPROACH (Later)

After connection is rebuilt.

Examples:

  • “remember earlier when i said i had something for u…”
  • “what u said earlier actually made me think of something”
