✨ The Golden Rule

A “no” is never the end of the conversation — it's a redirect.

When a fan says:

  • “too expensive”
  • “not now”
  • goes quiet after a PPV

Your next 2–3 messages decide whether the sale is lost or recovered.

📋 The 4 Types of Objections

1️⃣ Price Objection

“That’s too much” / “I’m broke”

What he really means:

“I need a reason to justify spending.”

🔴 WRONG

Him: thats too much babe

You: ok

You: [sends another PPV 5 minutes later]

Him: im broke rn

You: its only $20 tho

Him: nah thats expensive

You: 😢 i made it just for u tho

🟢 RIGHT

Him: thats too much babe

You: thats ok baby i totally get it

You: i just wanted u to see it first before anyone else

You: continue conversation normally

Him: im broke rn

You: omg dont even worry about it

You: i didnt send it for the money honestly

You: i just got so turned on thinking about what u said earlier

You: shift back to emotional connection

💡 Why it works

  • Removes pressure
  • Makes the content about him, not money
  • Keeps the door open for later purchase

never brings it up again

Him: not rn

🟢 RIGHT

💡 Why it works

Creates FOMO without pressure.

You:

  • plant curiosity

3️⃣ Goes Cold After PPV Drop

💡 Why it works

Connection increases → purchase likelihood increases.

This reframes the content as:

personal → exclusive → emotional

🔄 The 4-Step Objection Framework

Step 1 — ✅ ACKNOWLEDGE

Accept his response immediately.

Examples:

  • “omg totally get it”
  • “no worries at all babe”
  • “thats completely ok”

Step 2 — 🔁 REFRAME

Make it about connection, not money.

Examples:

  • “i just wanted u to see it first”
  • “i wasnt even thinking about the price honestly”

Step 3 — ↪️ REDIRECT

Return to personal conversation.

Examples:

  • “anyways tell me about ur day”
  • “did u end up going to that car show?”

Step 4 — 🎯 RE-APPROACH (Later)

After connection is rebuilt.

Examples:

  • “remember earlier when i said i had something for u…”
  • “what u said earlier actually made me think of something”
