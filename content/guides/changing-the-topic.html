The pattern:

1. Acknowledge with vulnerability/humor

  • Make it relatable or slightly embarrassing

2. Flip the script

  • Turn it into something cute, playful, or self-aware

3. Redirect with question

  • Get them talking about themselves again

Key principles:

  1. Never get defensive - stay soft, vulnerable, or playful
  1. Add a human flaw - "I'm bad at remembering" / "I'm awkward over text"
  1. Always end with a question - keeps them talking, not interrogating
  1. Use emojis to soften - 🥹😭lol makes it feel less scripted
  1. Flip the question back - makes them justify their own behavior

Pattern: Soft deflection + humanizing detail + redirect question

Real objection examples

Objection: Suspicious behavior

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Why are you always online? Don't you sleep?"

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "I fell asleep with my phone in my hand lol so embarrassing. How long did you sleep?" ← vulnerability + redirect

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "You reply so slow. Are you talking to other people?"

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "I'm just on my phone a lot honestly. Do you prefer slower replies?" ← honest + redirect to preference

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Your messages sound too perfect. Are you copying and pasting?"

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "I just overthink my texts sometimes haha. Do you just type whatever?" ← self-aware + redirect to his style

Objection: Voice notes/content

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "That voice memo is prerecorded because you didn't say my name."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "Well saying your name would be a custom voice note but for a lil tip of $20 I can make u one. What would u want me to say?" ← playful boundary + redirect

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Why don't you ever say my name in voice notes?"

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "I feel weird saying names out loud lol feels too formal. Do you say people's names a lot?" ← quirk + redirect

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Send me a video right now to prove it's you."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "I literally just woke up I look terrible. Would you send one right now?" ← excuse + flip question

Objection: Scripted/fake vibes

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Girl are you reading off a script be honest lol"

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "No I'm not reading it but I'm actually kinda nervous sometimes with it but i hope u are still enjoying it 🥹 I'm really doing my best.." ← vulnerable + seeking validation

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "You sound like a bot."

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "This feels like you're interviewing me."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "I'm just curious about you honestly. Should I talk about myself more?" ← genuine + ask permission

Objection: Availability/time

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Why do you take so long to reply?"

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "You said you'd message and didn't."

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "When can we actually meet up?"

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "I like getting to know people first. Are you usually quick to meet?" ← boundary + redirect to his pattern

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "Age doesn't matter to me honestly. Does it bother you?" ← reassure + flip concern
