The pattern:

Short answer → Pull thread → Pull again → Ask why/how/what → They open → Mirror → Pull deeper → Repeat

The key is never moving to a new topic until they've fully opened up on the current one.

Framework

Step 1. Short opener (≤7 words)

Step 2. They answer → Pull ONE thread

  • Why/How/What + [the thing they just said]

Step 3. Dig deeper into feeling/cause/impact

  • Feeling: "How'd that feel?" / "What's that like?"
  • Cause: "Why's that?" / "What made you that way?"
  • Impact: "How'd that change you?" / "What does that mean for you?"

Step 4. Mirror + Connect to values

  • "So [value/need/pattern] matters to you."

Step 5. Repeat step 2-4 until you understand them

Real examples (starting with short/disinterested answers)

Example 1: Getting past one-word answers

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "How was your day?" ← step 1

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Fine."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What did you do?" ← step 2 (pull thread)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Work."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "How was work?" ← step 2 (pull thread)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Long."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What made it long?" ← step 3 (cause)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Back to back meetings. No breaks."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "How'd that feel?" ← step 3 (feeling)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Draining honestly. I hate when I can't think."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "So you need space to think." ← step 4 (mirror value)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Yeah exactly. I can't function without downtime."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "Has it always been like that?" ← step 3 (cause/history)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Yeah. Even as a kid I needed alone time. My family was loud so I'd hide in my room."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What was that like for you?" ← step 3 (feeling)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Peaceful. Only place I felt calm."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "So calm matters a lot to you." ← step 4 (mirror value)

You learned: introvert, needs downtime, values calm/quiet, childhood shaped this.

Example 2: From closed to open

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What's on your mind lately?" ← step 1

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Nothing much."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What did you think about today?" ← step 2 (pull thread)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Work stuff."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What kind of work stuff?" ← step 2 (pull thread)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Whether to take this new role."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What's making you hesitate?" ← step 3 (cause)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "More money but way more stress. Not sure it's worth it."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What matters more to you?" ← step 3 (impact/values)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Honestly? Peace of mind. I'm tired of burning out."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "You've burned out before?" ← step 2 (pull thread)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Yeah. Three years ago. Worst time of my life."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What happened?" ← step 3 (cause/history)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "I was working 80 hour weeks. Relationship fell apart. Health got bad. Lost myself."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "How'd that change you?" ← step 3 (impact)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Made me realize money isn't everything. Now I protect my time."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "So balance really matters to you now." ← step 4 (mirror value)

You learned: burned out before, values peace > money, protects boundaries now, past trauma shaped priorities.

Example 3: Breaking through walls

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "Where are you from?" ← step 1

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Texas."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What part?" ← step 2 (pull thread)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Small town. You wouldn't know it."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "Did you like it there?" ← step 2 (pull thread)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "No."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "Why not?" ← step 3 (cause)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Everyone knew everyone. No privacy. Super judgmental."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What was that like for you?" ← step 3 (feeling)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Suffocating. I couldn't be myself."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What did you want to be?" ← step 2 (pull thread)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "I don't know. Just not what they expected."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What did they expect?" ← step 3 (cause)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Stay local. Get married. Work at the plant like my dad. Never leave."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "How'd that make you feel?" ← step 3 (feeling)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Trapped. Like my life was already decided."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "So freedom really matters to you." ← step 4 (mirror value)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Yeah. That's why I left. Never looked back."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "How'd leaving change you?" ← step 3 (impact)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Made me realize I can create my own path. No one else gets to decide."

You learned: values freedom, felt trapped growing up, broke expectations, independence is core identity.

Example 4: One-word to vulnerable

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What do you do for fun?" ← step 1

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Nothing really."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What do you do after work?" ← step 2 (pull thread)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Go home."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What's home like for you?" ← step 2 (pull thread)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Quiet."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "Is that what you want?" ← step 3 (feeling/values)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Yeah. Work's loud. I need quiet."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What makes work loud?" ← step 2 (pull thread)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "People always needing something. Never ends."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "How does that feel?" ← step 3 (feeling)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Exhausting. Sometimes I just want to disappear."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What do you mean by disappear?" ← step 2 (pull thread)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Just be alone. Not have to perform or be 'on' for anyone."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "So you feel like you're always performing." ← step 4 (mirror pattern)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Yeah. It's tiring pretending to be fine all the time."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "Are you not fine?" ← step 2 (pull thread)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Not really. Just going through motions lately."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What's making you feel that way?" ← step 3 (cause)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "I don't know. Feel stuck. Like nothing excites me anymore."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "Has something changed?" ← step 3 (cause/history)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "My divorce finalized last month. Think that's part of it."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "That sounds really hard." ← step 4 (mirror feeling)

You learned: burned out, feels like he's performing, going through divorce, emotionally stuck, craves authenticity.

Example 5: Work

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What do you do for work?" ← step 1

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "I’m a technician."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What kind of technician are you?" ← step 2

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "I fix machines in factories."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What’s that like for you?" ← step 3 (feeling)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Pretty stressful but satisfying when I fix something."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "So solving problems and getting things working again really matters to you." ← step 4 (mirror value)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Yeah, I like being that person."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "Have you always been the one people call when something’s broken?" ← step 3 (cause/history)

him 🙋🏽‍♂️: "Honestly, yeah, even in my family."

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "What’s that like for you, always being the fixer?" ← step 3 (feeling)

you 🙋🏼‍♀️: "So being reliable and useful is a big part of who you are." ← step 4 (mirror value)
