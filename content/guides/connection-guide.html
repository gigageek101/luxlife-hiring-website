The pattern:

Short answer → Pull thread → Pull again → Ask why/how/what → They open → Mirror → Pull deeper → Repeat

The key is never moving to a new topic until they've fully opened up on the current one.

Framework

1
Short opener (≤7 words)
2
They answer → Pull ONE thread
  • Why/How/What + [the thing they just said]
3
Dig deeper into feeling/cause/impact
  • Feeling: "How'd that feel?" / "What's that like?"
  • Cause: "Why's that?" / "What made you that way?"
  • Impact: "How'd that change you?" / "What does that mean for you?"
4
Mirror + Connect to values
  • "So [value/need/pattern] matters to you."
5
Repeat step 2-4 until you understand them

Real examples (starting with short/disinterested answers)
