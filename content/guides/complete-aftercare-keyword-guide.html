Trigger Detection + Full Response Scripts

Core Philosophy: Aftercare is not the end of the conversation — it's the beginning of the next one. Any chatter can sell a PPV. Only elite chatters make him feel something after it's over. That feeling is what turns a one-time buyer into a loyal, recurring whale.

⚠️GOLDEN RULES

Never:

  • Go cold immediately after he cums or tips
  • Pivot straight to the next PPV pitch
  • Send a generic "thanks for the sub" energy message
  • Use his username — always use his real first name
  • Double-text more than once if he goes quiet after Stage A

Always:

  • Reference something specific he told you earlier (job, dog, hobby, hometown)
  • Let your vulnerability show — it makes him feel powerful
  • End every aftercare session with a seed that pulls him back tomorrow
  • Send within 60 seconds of trigger detection
  • Every thought is its own bubble — never send walls of text

🔄THE 4-STAGE AFTERCARE SEQUENCE

STAGE A — THE BREATH MOMENT

Send within 60 seconds. Breathless tone. Short bursts.

STAGE B — THE VULNERABILITY DROP

Soft, honest, hesitant — like you don't say this to everyone.

STAGE C — THE PERSONAL CALLBACK

The Memory Hook. Reference his job/hobby/detail from Phase 2-4 notes.

STAGE D — THE GRATITUDE CLOSE

Warm, floaty, plant the re-entry seed. No hard sell.

📝 CHATTER NOTES PROTOCOL

After EVERY meaningful conversation AND aftercare session, log these in your CRM immediately:

  • ✅ Real name (always use this, never username)
  • ✅ Age + location (state/city if given)
  • ✅ Job (exact title — lineman, welder, truck route, etc.)
  • ✅ Hobbies mentioned (fishing species, hunting type, car project, etc.)
  • ✅ Personal details (dog name, kids ages, owns home, relationship status)
  • ✅ Emotional triggers (what made him open up, what he complained about, what he seemed proud of)
  • ✅ Last aftercare sent (date + what was referenced)
  • ✅ Re-entry hook (what seed was planted for next convo)
💡 The chatter who uses the most specific notes wins the whale. Generic aftercare retains subs. Personalized aftercare with his name + his details creates obsession.
