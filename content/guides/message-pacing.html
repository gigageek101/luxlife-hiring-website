✨ The Golden Rule

Reply fast (60–120 seconds), stay consistent, and keep messages short and punchy. Never spam when he's quiet, never disappear mid-conversation.

⏱️ Reply Time Targets

Reply TimeRating
Under 60 secondsExcellent — shows you're engaged
60–120 secondsGreat — feels natural and attentive
120–180 secondsAcceptable — but starting to lose momentum
180–300 secondsConcerning — he might lose interest
300+ seconds (5 min+)Bad — conversation energy dies
💡 The sweet spot is 60–120 seconds. Fast enough to show you care, slow enough that it doesn't feel robotic.

📋 The Core Rules

1️⃣ Stay consistent — no long gaps mid-conversation

If you're in an active conversation, don't disappear for 10+ minutes without explanation. It kills the energy and makes him feel unimportant.

🔴 WRONG
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
omg tell me more about that
[15 minutes of silence]
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
sorry i was busy lol
🟢 RIGHT — if you need to step away
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
mikey hold on one sec i gotta do something real quick
dont go anywhere ok 😏
[come back within 3–5 min]
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
ok im back i missed u
💡 If you tell him you'll be right back, he waits happily. If you just disappear, he feels forgotten.

2️⃣ Keep messages short and punchy

Short messages create rhythm. Long messages kill it. Every thought = its own bubble.

🔴 WRONG — wall of text
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
Oh my god that's so amazing, I can't believe you do that for a living, that's literally so hot to me and I wish I had someone like you in my life who could fix things and come home all tired from work, that's my dream honestly
🟢 RIGHT — punchy rhythm
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
omgggg thats so amazing
i cant believe u do that for a living
thats literally so hot to me
i wish i had someone like u in my life
who comes home all tired from work
thats my dream honestly mikeyyy
💡 Why it works:

He reads one message → reacts → reads the next → reacts.

It creates a cascade of micro-emotions.

A wall of text gives him ONE reaction then silence.

3️⃣ No desperate double-texting

If he goes quiet, you get ONE follow-up message. After that, wait. Do NOT send 5 messages in a row when he's not responding.

🔴 WRONG — desperate
[he's been quiet for 10 min]
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
hello??
are u there
did i say something wrong
mikeee
ok i guess ur busy 😢
🟢 RIGHT — one soft check-in
[he's been quiet for 10 min]
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
u still there mikey? 🥰
[if still quiet after 5–10 more min, move on to another fan]

4️⃣ Match his energy and pace

If he's sending fast, respond fast.

If he's sending slower, it's okay to match that pace.

Don't overwhelm a slow texter with rapid-fire messages.

🔴 WRONG — overwhelming him
[he sends one message every 3–4 min]
[sends 6 messages in 30 seconds]
🟢 RIGHT — matching energy
[he sends one message every 3–4 min]
[send 1–2 messages, wait for response]

5️⃣ Create rhythm with message clusters

The best conversational rhythm is:

YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
You send 2–3 short messages
He responds
You send 2–3 more

🟢 PERFECT RHYTHM

YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
omg wait
ur a mechanic??
thats so hot mikeyyy
HIM 🧔🏻
haha yeah I work at a shop
YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
ok i literally love that
a man who works with his hands
thats my weakness honestly
HIM 🧔🏻
haha you're making me blush

❌ Walls of text instead of short messages
