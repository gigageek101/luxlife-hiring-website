✨ The Golden Rule

If it feels like you're selling something, you're doing it wrong. Every PPV, every tip, every purchase should happen because the emotional connection made it feel natural — not because you pushed for it.

📋 The Line Between Selling and Connecting

The difference between a great chatter and a bad one isn't whether they make sales — it's whether the fan FEELS sold to.

📜 The 6 Rules of Professional Compliance

Rule 1: Never beg for purchases

The moment you say "please buy" or "please open" — you've broken the illusion. Content should sell itself through the emotional connection you built.

Rule 2: Never spam PPVs without conversation

Every PPV must be preceded by genuine conversation. If you haven't talked to him in 10+ messages, you haven't earned the right to send a PPV.

Rule 3: Never go cold after purchases

Buying content is an intimate moment. If you immediately go transactional or silent after he buys, you've told him he's just a wallet.

Rule 4: Never guilt-trip

"I worked so hard on this" / "it would mean so much" / "nobody appreciates me" — these are manipulation tactics that destroy trust.

Rule 5: Never use fake urgency

"Deleting in 1 hour" / "limited time" / "only sending to 3 people" — fans see through this. It feels like a used car salesman, not a girlfriend.

Rule 6: Emotional connection comes first, always

If you're choosing between making a sale NOW and preserving the relationship for tomorrow — always choose the relationship.

Long-term fans are worth 10× more than one-time buyers.

⚖️ The Balance Test

Before sending any PPV or making any sales-adjacent move, ask yourself:

"Would a real girlfriend do this?"

Would a real girlfriend say:

YOU 🙋🏼‍♀️
please buy my video

No.

Would a real girlfriend share something intimate because the moment felt right?

Yes.

Would a real girlfriend go cold after her boyfriend did something nice?

No.

Would a real girlfriend guilt-trip him into spending money?

No.

Would a real girlfriend bring up something she made for him because she was thinking about him?

Yes.

If the answer is:

"a real girlfriend would NOT do this"

Don't do it.
