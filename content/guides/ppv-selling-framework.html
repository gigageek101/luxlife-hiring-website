✨ The Golden Rule

A PPV should feel like a gift you're sharing because he made you feel something — not a product you're selling to hit a target.

💡 Minimum spacing:

At least 8–10 messages of real conversation between PPVs.

The conversation IS the sales tool.

🔥 The Full Vault Sequence

The ideal full session with a buying fan:

  1. Teasing Video 1 (free or low-price tease to build interest)
  1. Voice Memo 1 → PPV 1 ($20)
  1. Conversation, tension building, validation
  1. Voice Memo 2 → PPV 2 ($40)
  1. Conversation, aftercare from PPV 2, more connection
  1. Voice Memo 3 → PPV 3 ($60)
  1. Conversation, aftercare
  1. Voice Memo 4 → PPV 4 ($80)
  1. Full aftercare sequence

⚠️ Critical Mistakes

❌ Never send PPV without conversation first

If you haven't built rapport and connection, the PPV will feel like spam.

❌ Never send PPV with a long sales pitch

The voice memo does the selling.

The PPV itself should be clean.

❌ Never send multiple PPVs without conversation between them

Spacing is everything. Conversation builds the desire that makes PPVs sell.

❌ Never start at the highest price

$20 first. Always. Escalate from there.

❌ Never send the same price repeatedly

Escalating prices create a sense of progression and increasing value.
